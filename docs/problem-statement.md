# Problem Statement: Threat Intelligence Alert Fatigue

## The Core Problem
Defence analysts are currently overwhelmed by the sheer volume of daily security alerts generated by SIEM systems, satellite feeds, and cyber sensors. These alerts come in varying formats, making it impossible for a human team to manually read, correlate, and investigate every single one. 

## The Impact
1. **Missed Threats:** The "needle in a haystack" problem means a genuine, catastrophic advanced persistent threat (APT) can easily be missed among the noise of routine network pings.
2. **Resource Drain:** Chasing false positives wastes critical analyst time and operational resources.
3. **Delayed Decision Making:** Military commanders cannot read raw logs. They require structured, Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF) summaries to make rapid, tactical decisions. Currently, manually translating technical logs into BLUF reports takes hours, by which point the adversary has already pivoted through the network.
